How collision detection works

Two boxes collide only if they overlap on every axis: x, y, and z.

player = the green box (your cube) obstacle = the orange box (the thing flying at you)

Front view (x and y only). Drag either box. Use the z slider to move the obstacle nearer or farther.



Half-size = the distance from a box's center to its edge. The player's half-size is 1 here (0.7 in the game). The obstacle's half-width is its width ÷ 2, and so on.
